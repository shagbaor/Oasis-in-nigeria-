Who we are

An oasis of safety, dignity and hope

We believe that inclusion is not a privilege — it is a right. OASIS works alongside individuals and communities who are too often left out, to create environments where everyone is seen, respected and supported.

Through outreach, education, advocacy and community-led programmes, we connect people to the resources, networks and opportunities they need to live with dignity, while caring for the environment that sustains us all.

Our Mission

To build affirming spaces and inclusive communities through outreach, empowerment and sustainable action.

Our Vision

A society where every person can live openly, safely and fully, in harmony with their community and environment.

What we do

Four pillars, one purpose

Our programmes are built around the four commitments in our name.

Outreach

We go where people are — meeting communities directly with support, information and connection to services.

Affirming Spaces

We create and support safe physical and online spaces where people can show up as themselves without fear.

Inclusion

Through training, dialogue and advocacy, we help institutions and communities become more welcoming to all.

Sustainability

We support livelihoods, wellbeing and environmental stewardship so that communities can thrive long-term.
